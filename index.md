# Marketing Workspace

The memory of a marketing campaign, kept by your agent: your positioning, launch
venues, people and leads, posts and replies, mentions, readings, plan, and
backlog. The agent reads it to decide what to do next and writes back what
happened, so every session picks up where the last one stopped.

## Start here

1. Ask the agent to **run the setup**. It interviews you about the product, the
   ideal customer, pricing, and your stage, writes the
   [product](data/product.md) and [strategy](data/strategy.md) docs, and prunes
   the seeded launch venues to the ones that fit your customer.
2. Ask it to **backfill the workspace**. It researches what already exists —
   your website, mentions, published posts, competitors — and files each find
   where the graph expects it, asking you when it can't verify something.
3. After that, just talk to it: *"what should I do next?"*, *"draft a Show HN
   post"*, *"log this mention"*, *"I published it, here's the link"*, *"find
   threads worth answering"*, and once a week, *"weekly digest"*.

## Where things live

- [Product](data/product.md) — what you sell, to whom, and the messaging pillars
  every draft derives from.
- [Strategy](data/strategy.md) — the campaign's current stage and active
  channels.
- [Plan](data/plan.md) — campaign-level moves, each with its definition of done.
- [Backlog](data/backlog.md) — single-sitting tasks, linked by state.
- [Communities](data/communities.md) — every venue the campaign targets, with
  its posting rules.
- [People](data/people.md) — customers, prospects, and amplifiers, one document
  each.
- [Mentions](data/mentions.md) — every third-party mention of the product.
- [Experiments](data/experiments.md) — everything tried and its verdict.
- [Threads](data/threads.md), [Replies](data/replies.md) and
  [Leads](data/leads.md) — the conversations you join in other people's threads,
  what you said, how it landed, and who you met there.
- [Audiences](data/audiences.md) and [Selling points](data/selling-points.md) —
  who you talk to, and the arguments, with proof, that every draft starts from.
- [Readings](data/readings.md) — what you think the record means, kept apart
  from the facts, with the evidence that would change your mind.
- [Notes](data/notes.md), [References](data/references.md),
  [Emails](data/emails.md) and [Weekly reports](data/weekly.md) — decisions and
  learnings, outside ideas, outreach email, and the weeks you kept on file.

The [graph root](data/index.md) links every hub. [Structure](STRUCTURE.md)
explains the layout, [Schema](SCHEMA.md) lists the frontmatter each kind of
document carries, and the [agent manual](AGENTS.md) is what the agent follows.
