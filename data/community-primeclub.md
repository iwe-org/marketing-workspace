# Prime Club

A curated lifetime-deal marketplace that positions itself upmarket: it claims to
list only vetted, already-successful SaaS products and sells to a paying
membership of buyers. The vetting is a real gate (multi-round testing before
approval) but also a credibility signal if you pass, and a members-only audience
may churn-shop less than open deal sites. Still an LTD platform at heart — deep
discount, lifetime obligation — just with a smaller, more selective crowd than
AppSumo.
