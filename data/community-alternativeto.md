# AlternativeTo

A long-running, crowd-sourced directory built around one question: "what can I
use instead of X?" Its "alternatives to" pages rank very well in search,
delivering steady high-intent traffic to listed apps — visitors are actively
shopping for a replacement. Listing is free and worth doing carefully (good
screenshots, accurate alternative links to the incumbents you compete with), but
ranking within a page comes from genuine user likes over time, not from the
maker.
