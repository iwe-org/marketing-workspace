# SaaS Warrior

A Facebook group where lifetime-deal buyers discuss, vet, and share SaaS deals.

a promo post or AMA rather than dropping a link — unapproved self-promotion
typically gets removed. Useful as a low-cost amplifier around an LTD launch
running elsewhere; the audience is deal hunters, with all that implies for
conversion quality.
