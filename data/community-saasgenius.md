# SaaS Genius

A business-software review directory. The free-listing page still exists but the
site says it has stopped accepting free submissions due to volume, so getting
listed now means paying an undisclosed fee and meeting their B2B/US/post-beta
criteria. As a long-tail directory with modest traffic, this is hard to justify
at any meaningful price — reasonable to skip unless the fee turns out to be
trivial and the product squarely fits their B2B profile.
