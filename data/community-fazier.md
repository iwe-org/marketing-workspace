# Fazier

Fazier is a newer Product Hunt-style directory that surfaces a batch of products
each day with upvotes and daily rankings. Its audience is small and skews toward
other makers rather than end customers, so treat it as a low-effort secondary
listing: worth the ten minutes for the backlink and an embeddable launch badge,
but do not expect meaningful traffic or feedback from it. Slot it anywhere in
the sequence after your primary launches.
