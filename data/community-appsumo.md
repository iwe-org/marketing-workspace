# AppSumo

The largest lifetime-deal marketplace by a wide margin — a well-run AppSumo
launch can put a product in front of hundreds of thousands of deal buyers and
generate real cash fast. The trade-offs are equally large: you sell lifetime
access at a steep one-time discount, share a big cut with the platform, absorb a
heavy support and refund load, and inherit a deal-hunter cohort that rarely
converts to recurring revenue and can strain your roadmap forever. Use it
deliberately — for cash, users, and feedback at a specific stage — not as a
default growth channel.
