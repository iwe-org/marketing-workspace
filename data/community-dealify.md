# Dealify

A lifetime-deal platform with a lean toward growth-hacking and marketing tools,
selling deep-discount LTD campaigns to its buyer list. Reach sits well below
AppSumo, making it a secondary or niche-fit channel: worth considering if the
product targets marketers, less so otherwise. Standard LTD economics apply —
one-time revenue, lifetime obligations, and a deal-driven audience.
