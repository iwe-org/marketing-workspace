# Toolfolio

A general tool-discovery directory that organizes software across roughly 18
categories (design, AI, no-code, marketing, and similar). It is a long-tail
listing site: submitting is cheap and gets you a backlink and a small amount of
browse traffic, but it will not move signups on its own. Worth a one-time
submission when doing a directory sweep, not worth paying for a boost unless you
have evidence its audience matches yours.
