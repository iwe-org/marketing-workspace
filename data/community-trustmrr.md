# TrustMRR

Less a software directory than a marketplace of verified-revenue SaaS
businesses: startups connect a payment provider and their MRR, growth rate, and
valuation multiples are shown publicly, including to prospective buyers. The
audience is indie hackers, deal watchers, and acquirers — not end users — so
this drives credibility and acquisition interest, not signups. Only worth it if
you are comfortable publishing revenue figures; that transparency is the entire
value proposition.
