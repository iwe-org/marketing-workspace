# toools.design

A curated, weekly-updated directory of 2,200+ design resources and tools aimed
squarely at designers. Because it is curated rather than open-listing,
acceptance depends on genuinely fitting a design workflow — a developer tool
with no design angle will likely be ignored. When the fit is real, the audience
is well targeted and the curation gives a listing more weight than a typical
open directory.
