daily upvote leaderboard, and a top-five finish still delivers a real traffic
spike, press attention, and lasting SEO value. It is also the most competitive
and most gamed venue here — polished teams schedule launches weeks out, prepare
galleries and videos, and mobilize their networks for the early-morning Pacific

a launch sequence, after the smaller directories have warmed up your copy and
