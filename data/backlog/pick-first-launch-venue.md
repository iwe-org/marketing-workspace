# Pick and schedule the first launch venue

From the pruned shortlist, pick the first venue to launch on — usually a
low-stakes one (BetaList, a fitting subreddit) before the flagship (Product
Hunt, Show HN). Read the venue's `notes` for rules, put a date on it, and ask
the agent to draft the post (the draft skill pulls positioning + venue rules
together).
