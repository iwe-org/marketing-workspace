# Prune the venue list to your ICP

Walk the seeded venues
(`iwe find --filter '{type: community, status: planned}'`) against your ideal
customer profile. Set `status: cancelled` with a one-line reason in `notes` for
anything that doesn't reach your customer (wrong platform ecosystem, wrong
region, deal-hunter audience you don't want). Aim to keep a shortlist of ~10 you
will actually work — a short list you execute beats a long one you don't.
