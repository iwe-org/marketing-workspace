# Seed the CRM with people you already know

Add the first ~10 people to `data/people/`: existing users, friendly testers,
anyone who ever said something nice about the product, and the two or three
community voices your audience listens to. Give each a role via the role hubs
(customer, prospect, amplifier, creator, contact) and a `status`. Warm
relationships you already have are the cheapest marketing channel you own.
