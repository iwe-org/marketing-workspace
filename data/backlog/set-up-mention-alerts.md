# Set up mention alerts

Arrange to hear about it when someone mentions your product or your problem
space: keyword alerts on the platforms your audience uses (free tools exist for
Hacker News and Reddit tracking), plus a periodic search on your product name.
Record what you track in the `## Watchlist` section of `data/mentions.md`, and
file every real mention as a `data/mentions/` entry — a positive mention is
outreach warmed up for you.
