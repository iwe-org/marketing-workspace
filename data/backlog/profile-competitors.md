# Profile your top competitors

Create `data/competitors/<slug>.md` for the 2–4 alternatives named in
`data/product.md`'s Competitive landscape section: positioning, pricing,
strengths, weaknesses, and a dated first snapshot in the changelog. These
profiles feed comparison pages, pricing decisions, and SEO analysis — and a
skill pack's competitor skills can do the research pass for you.
