# SourceForge

A veteran open-source hosting site that has grown a large B2B software
comparison directory on top. Traffic is still massive (tens of millions of
downloads weekly), though the audience skews toward downloaders of established
desktop/server software rather than early adopters. For an open-source project,
a free mirror listing costs little and adds a distribution point plus a credible
backlink; for commercial vendors it is another review-carrying directory to
claim rather than a growth lever.
