# Get the first 10 users

Nothing at this stage scales, and that's the point. The first ten users are
found by hand, one conversation at a time — you're validating that the problem
is real, the positioning lands, and someone will change their behavior to use
what you built. Talk to people directly, watch them struggle, and file every
conversation as an interview record (`data/interviews/`), distilling what you
learn into `data/product.md` (positioning, customer language) and the CRM
(people) — the learnings here feed every later stage.

The moves: work your existing network and the communities where your ideal
customer already asks questions (social listening); recruit via direct messages
and beta-user venues; do things that feel embarrassingly manual, like onboarding
each user personally.

## Definition of done

- [ ] 10 people have actually used the product (not signed up — used)
- [ ] Each has a CRM entry with role `customer` and what you learned from them
- [ ] `data/product.md` positioning rewritten at least once from real user
  language
- [ ] At least 3 users you didn't previously know
