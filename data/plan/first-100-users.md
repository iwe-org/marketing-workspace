# Get the first 100 users

The first hundred come from showing up where early adopters already gather:
launch platforms, communities, and your own build-in-public trail. The work is
still manual but now public — every launch, post, and reply is also an asset
that compounds. Expect spiky traffic, thin conversion, and priceless feedback;
the goal is a small group of people who *use* the product, not a big number who
saw it once.

The moves: work the seeded venue checklist (`data/communities.md`) — launch
platforms first, then the subreddits that fit your ICP; one real launch (Product
Hunt or Show HN) with proper preparation; keep a build-in-public cadence on the
channel your audience reads. Record every post in the pipeline and every warm
reply in the CRM.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Launched on at least 5 venues from the checklist (each flipped to
  `active`)
- [ ] One prepared flagship launch (Product Hunt or Show HN) done
- [ ] 100 users; the acquisition venue recorded for each cohort in
  `data/strategy.md` notes
- [ ] At least 10 mentions/comments from strangers filed in `data/mentions/`
