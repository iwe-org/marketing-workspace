# Indie Hackers (products)

Indie Hackers is a long-running community of bootstrapped founders, and its
products directory lets you create a page for your product and attach milestone
posts, revenue updates, and progress logs to it. The listing alone brings almost
no traffic — the venue rewards sustained participation, and a well-told
milestone can reach the front page and a large founder audience. Treat it as a
slow channel you join before launch and keep feeding afterward, not a launch-day
event; the audience is founders and makers, which is ideal if they are your
customers and merely sympathetic if they are not.
