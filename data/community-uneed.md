# Uneed

Uneed is an indie-run daily launchpad that has become one of the better Product
Hunt alternatives: a limited number of products launch per day, the community
upvotes them, and daily winners get badges. It reaches the indie-hacker and
maker crowd — good for early feedback, a solid backlink, and some day-one
traffic, though nowhere near Product Hunt or Show HN in reach. Because the free
queue can be long, submit early and let it land between your bigger launches; it
is a genuine mid-tier venue, not just a link-farm.
