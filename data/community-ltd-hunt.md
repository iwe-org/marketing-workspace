# LTD Hunt

The Facebook community around LTD Hunt, an aggregator that tracks lifetime deals
across platforms. Members are habitual LTD buyers comparing active deals, so it
works as a discovery and amplification layer when you already have a live deal —
on AppSumo, a secondary platform, or self-hosted. Little value outside an active
deal window, and the audience optimizes for price over retention.
