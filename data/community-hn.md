# Hacker News (Show HN)

Show HN is the section of Hacker News for things you built that others can try,
and it offers the largest and most technical audience of any venue on this list.
A post that lands can bring tens of thousands of visitors, sharp feedback, and
follow-on press; most posts sink without a trace, so the variance is extreme.
Save it for when the product genuinely works, write a plain-spoken title and a
first comment explaining what and why, and expect blunt criticism — the audience
rewards substance and punishes marketing language. Best placed at the center of
a launch sequence, on its own day.
