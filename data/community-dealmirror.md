# DealMirror

An established lifetime-deal store selling one-time-payment access to SaaS
across marketing, AI, e-commerce, and developer categories. It has a loyal
deal-hunter following and functions as a common second stop for products
extending an LTD run beyond a flagship platform. The usual caveats hold: deep
discounts, perpetual support obligations to one-time buyers, and buyers who
follow deals rather than products.
