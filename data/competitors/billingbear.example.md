## Positioning

Web-first invoicing suite for freelancers and small agencies — "everything in
one place": invoicing, expenses, time tracking. Sells breadth; onboarding
funnels users toward the full suite immediately.

## Pricing

Free tier (2 clients, branded invoices) → $12/mo Solo → $29/mo Team. Value
metric is clients + seats. Annual discount 20%.

## Strengths

Integrations (Stripe, QuickBooks, 20+ others); polished mobile app; strong SEO
on "freelance invoice template" queries.

## Weaknesses

Slow, browser-only workflow (our wedge); users complain the invoice editor takes
six clicks to do what `pi new` does in one prompt; no API on Solo tier.
