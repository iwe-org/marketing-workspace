# StackSocial

The flagship storefront of StackCommerce, a commerce platform selling discounted
software, app subscriptions, gadgets, and courses — including lifetime-license
deals. Its distinguishing feature is distribution: accepted deals can syndicate
across the branded shops StackCommerce runs for large media publishers,
multiplying reach beyond the main store. The audience is broad consumer
bargain-hunters rather than SaaS-specific buyers, and the usual LTD economics
(deep discount, platform cut, low recurring conversion) apply.
