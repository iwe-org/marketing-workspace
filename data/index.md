# Foundation

- [Product](product.md) - What the product is, who it is for, and the messaging
  pillars every draft derives from.
- [Strategy](strategy.md) - The current campaign posture: stage, active
  channels, and what is deliberately not being worked.
- [Brand](brand.md) - Voice, visuals, and the reusable description variants copy
  and creative work start from.
- [Offer](offer.md) - What is sold and on what terms — the canonical answer for
  pricing, trial, and guarantee.

# Pillars

- [Invoice without leaving the
  terminal](pillars/invoice-without-leaving-terminal.example)
  - Example pillar hub collecting every publication that carries this messaging
    pillar.

# State

- [Metrics](metrics.md) - The campaign's baseline numbers and the dated
  snapshots that show movement.
- [Website](website.md) - The web properties' living record — what exists, what
  it targets, and what still needs writing.

# Execution

- [Plan](plan.md) - Campaign-level moves, each a plan step with its own
  definition of done.
- [Backlog](backlog.md) - Atomic, single-sitting tasks, linked here by state.
- [Experiments](experiments.md) - Everything tried and its verdict — the
  permanent answer to "what have you already tested".
- [Posts](posts.md) - Published posts at a glance — engagement, and whether each
  met its success line.

# Entities

- [Communities](communities.md) - Every venue this campaign targets or tracks,
  from launch platforms to subreddits.
- [People](people.md) - The campaign's CRM — one document per person, grouped by
  the role they play.
- [Competitors](competitors.md) - One profile per competitor, refreshed through
  dated snapshots.
- [Mentions](mentions.md) - Every third-party mention of the product — comments,
  threads, listings, reviews.
- [Interviews](interviews.md) - Deliberate research events — customer interviews
  and surveys, one dated record each.
- [Audiences](audiences.md) - The audience segments you market to — what lands
  with each and where they gather.
- [Selling points](selling-points.md) - The concrete reasons to buy, each with
  its proof and the copy it feeds, grouped by kind.

# Conversations

- [Threads](threads.md) - Every thread where you met people — the conversations
  your replies sit in.
- [Replies](replies.md) - Every comment you posted in someone else's thread,
  judged by outcome rather than score.
- [Leads](leads.md) - People met in threads who may want the product, one record
  each until they graduate to the CRM.
- [Emails](emails.md) - Outreach email sent and received, one record each, with
  goal, success and outcome.

# Learning

- [Readings](readings.md) - What you currently think the campaign record means,
  one reading each, by impact.
- [Readings archive](readings-archive.md) - Readings no longer waiting for
  evidence, kept as the record of what was decided and why.
- [References](references.md) - Outside material you take marketing ideas from,
  with the ideas written down.
- [Notes](notes.md) - Short durable notes — decisions, learnings and gotchas a
  future session should know.
- [Weekly reports](weekly.md) - Weekly digests you chose to keep on file, newest
  first.
