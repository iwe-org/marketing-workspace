# Peerlist

Peerlist is a professional network for tech people (a
designer/developer-flavored LinkedIn alternative), and its Launchpad feature
runs weekly product launch cycles where the community upvotes entries and
winners get badges. The audience is genuinely engaged — working developers and
designers rather than drive-by voters — but you need a real Peerlist profile
with some presence for a launch to land. Worth using mid-sequence if your
product targets developers or designers; skip it for consumer products, where
the audience mismatch makes it low-yield.
