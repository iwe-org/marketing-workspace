# Microlaunch

Microlaunch runs month-long launch cohorts instead of the usual 24-hour
leaderboard, which gives slow-burn products more time to accumulate votes and
feedback. The audience is a small circle of indie makers and early-adopter
types, so the realistic payoff is peer feedback, a backlink, and a possible
product-of-the-month badge rather than customer traffic. Use it as a low-cost
supplementary listing early in the sequence; the format is friendlier than a
one-day contest, but the reach is limited.
