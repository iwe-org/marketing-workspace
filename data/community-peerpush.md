# PeerPush

PeerPush is a product discovery directory built around explicit reciprocity:
your listing's visibility is driven by a score you earn through mutual support —
upvoting, commenting on, and sharing other builders' products — with daily,
weekly, and monthly awards for the most active. That design means the audience
is almost entirely other founders working the same system, so expect peer
networking and a backlink rather than customer traffic. The free queue makes it
a reasonable zero-cost checklist item late in the sequence; the paid boosts are
hard to justify unless fellow builders are literally your target market.
