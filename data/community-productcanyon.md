# ProductCanyon

A lifetime- and annual-deal marketplace for business software, pitching one-time
payment access instead of subscriptions. It sits in the mid-to-long tail of LTD
platforms: live listings and testimonials show a functioning operation, but
reach is a fraction of AppSumo's. Sensible as a secondary outlet for an existing
deal, with the standard LTD trade-offs of discounted lifetime buyers and
platform revenue share.
