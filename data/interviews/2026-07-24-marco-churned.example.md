30-minute call with [Marco](../people/marco.example), who cancelled PlainInvoice
after two months. Not a product rejection — a workflow mismatch: his agency grew
and bookkeeping moved to an assistant who doesn't live in the terminal.

Verbatim quotes worth keeping:

- "It was perfect when it was just me. The moment someone else had to send
  invoices, the terminal became a wall."
- "I'd come back for solo side work in a heartbeat."

Learnings: the free→paid boundary holds for solo freelancers but breaks at first
hire — either a web view for delegates someday, or position explicitly as
solo-only and say so on the pricing page. Filed the pricing-page wording as an
