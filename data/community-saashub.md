# SaaSHub

An independent software marketplace and alternatives directory with well over
200k products listed. Its value is long-tail SEO: "X alternatives" and
comparison pages rank and send a slow trickle of high-intent visitors over time.
A free listing is low effort and worth claiming and verifying; do not expect a
launch spike — this is a compounding-background-traffic play, not an
announcement channel.
