# Freelance backend developers

*Example document — this shows the shape of an audience segment: what lands with
them, what to avoid, and links to the pitches and venues that reach them. The
setup skill can create one per segment named in the interview, and deletes
`*.example.md` files after onboarding.*

## Who they are

Solo developers billing three to ten clients a month, working in a terminal all
day. They start looking when a client disputes an invoice or a web tool raises
its price.

## What resonates

Plain files, git history, no extra login. Phrases they use: "invoicing that
lives where I work", "versions like code".

## What to avoid

Feature lists, "all-in-one", anything that sounds like a finance product.
