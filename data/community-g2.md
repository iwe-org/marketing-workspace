# G2

The dominant B2B software review platform — a profile here is table stakes for
anything sold to businesses, and G2 category pages rank hard in search. It is
review-gated: an empty profile does almost nothing, so the real work is running
review-collection campaigns with actual users, and the paid vendor tiers are
priced for funded companies. Worth claiming the free profile early and steadily
accumulating reviews; skip the paid tiers until there is revenue to justify
them.
