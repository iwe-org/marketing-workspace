# OpenAlternative

A directory of open-source alternatives to popular proprietary software.
Visitors arrive specifically looking to replace a commercial tool, so intent is
high, and listings surface live GitHub health metrics rather than marketing
copy. One of the better free listings for an open-source dev tool; irrelevant
for closed-source products, and a neglected repo (stale commits, few stars) will
show poorly.
