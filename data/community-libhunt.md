# LibHunt

A developer-facing platform that tracks open-source projects and libraries and
ranks them by mentions on social networks (notably Hacker News and Reddit) and
repository activity. The audience is developers comparing libraries and hunting
alternatives, and LibHunt also runs per-ecosystem newsletters that can surface
trending projects. Listing is free and worth doing for any open-source project,
but visibility follows real-world discussion — it rewards projects that are
already being talked about rather than creating attention.
