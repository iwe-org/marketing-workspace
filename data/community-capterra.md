# Capterra

One of the big three B2B software review directories (with G2 and Software
Advice), owned by Gartner. Like G2 it is review-gated — placement and
credibility come from verified reviews, and prominent category positions are
effectively bought via per-click bidding — so a free listing without reviews
sits deep in the list. Reviews collected here typically syndicate across
Gartner's sibling sites (GetApp, Software Advice), which makes the
review-collection effort go further. Claim the listing, but treat paid placement
as a later, budget-dependent decision.
