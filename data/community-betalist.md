# BetaList

BetaList is one of the oldest startup discovery sites, focused specifically on
pre-launch and beta-stage products, and it reaches early adopters who enjoy
trying things before they are polished. It fits at the very start of a launch
sequence — list here while you are still collecting beta signups, well before
Product Hunt or Show HN. Expect a modest trickle of curious signups and a decent
backlink rather than a traffic spike; the site is respectable but its heyday as
a discovery channel has passed.
