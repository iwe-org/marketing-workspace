## What it is

A teardown of twelve command-line tools' READMEs, ranking them by how fast a
reader sees the tool do its job. The winners open with a short looping GIF of
one real command, before any install step.

## Ideas we can use

- Lead the launch post with a GIF of `pi new`, not a video (from the full

  the video went unwatched.
- Put the install line under the GIF, never above it (from the full article).
