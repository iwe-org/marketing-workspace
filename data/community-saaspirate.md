# SaaSPirate

An aggregator of lifetime deals and founder-provided discounts for SaaS and AI
tools. Because many listings are direct founder coupons rather than platform-run
campaigns, the barrier to entry is low and you keep more control over terms —
but so is the reach, and the audience is the same price-first LTD crowd. A cheap
add-on during a deal window; not a primary channel.
