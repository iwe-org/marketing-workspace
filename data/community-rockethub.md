# RocketHub

A mid-size lifetime-deal platform aimed at entrepreneurs and small businesses
buying SaaS at one-time prices. Smaller reach than AppSumo means a smaller cash
spike but also less overwhelming support load, and secondary LTD platforms like
this are sometimes used to extend a deal after a flagship launch. Same
structural caveats as any LTD: lifetime obligations to discount buyers and an
audience optimizing for deals rather than long-term fit.
