# SideProjectors

SideProjectors is first and foremost a marketplace where makers sell side
projects or look for co-founders, with a showcase option bolted on for projects
that are not for sale. The audience is other makers and small-time acquirers
browsing for deals, so showcasing here yields a backlink and occasional maker
eyeballs, not customers. It is a marginal fit for a launch checklist — include
it only as a five-minute completeness item, or keep it in mind for later if you
ever want to sell the project.
