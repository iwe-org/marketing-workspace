# SaasZilla

A smaller lifetime-deal storefront in the long tail of LTD platforms. Sites at
this scale offer little standalone reach; their use case is extending an LTD
campaign's footprint after the main launch, or testing deal pricing quietly
before a bigger platform push. Vet the operator (payout terms, support
expectations, deal duration) carefully — small LTD platforms vary widely in
professionalism.
