# There's An AI For That

One of the largest AI-tool directories (TAAFT), with substantial organic traffic
from people searching for an AI tool for a specific task. Only relevant if the
product can honestly be framed as an AI tool — stretching to fit will get lost
among thousands of listings anyway. The audience skews toward casual
tool-samplers, so expect clicks more than committed users; whether a paid
listing pays back depends on how directly the product matches a task people
search for there.
