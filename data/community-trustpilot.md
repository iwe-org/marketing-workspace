# Trustpilot

A consumer-facing review platform rather than a software directory: people
search it to check whether a company is trustworthy before buying. It only pays
off once there are happy customers to invite, since an unclaimed or empty
profile — or a profile with a stray angry review — can actively hurt. Best
treated as reputation infrastructure to set up once real users exist, not as a
discovery or launch channel.
