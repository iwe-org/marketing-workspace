# Softonic

A very large consumer software download portal covering Windows, Mac, Android,
and iOS apps and games. Its enormous traffic is consumer-grade and
download-oriented — people grabbing installers, not evaluating B2B or developer
tooling — so it mainly suits desktop apps with mass-market appeal. For a niche
SaaS or developer tool the audience mismatch makes this low priority despite the
raw numbers.
