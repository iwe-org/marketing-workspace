Context: exit interviews and two HN comments both flagged the "is this a real

`data/product.md` into a strip under the hero.

## Learning

Named testimonials with real client counts beat logos: the variant with "Sarah
K., 31 invoices sent" outperformed the logo-strip variant we tried first.
Reusable pattern — specificity is the persuasive ingredient, not the presence of
social proof.
