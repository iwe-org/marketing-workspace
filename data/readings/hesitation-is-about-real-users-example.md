# Visitors hesitate because they can't tell whether real freelancers use PlainInvoice

*Example document — this shows a reading after its deciding evidence came in:
`stage: supported`, linked from the readings archive instead of the readings
hub. It stays as the record of what was decided and on what facts. The setup
skill deletes `*-example.md` files after onboarding.*

## Facts

- Two trial users said in exit notes they "weren't sure anyone actually uses
  this" (June 2026).
- The [social-proof experiment](../experiments/landing-social-proof-example.md)
  moved visitor-to-signup conversion from 2.1% to 2.9% over three weeks.
- The winning variant named a customer and a count ("Sarah K., 31 invoices
  sent"); the logo-strip variant tried first lifted less.

## Reading

The hesitation is about whether peers use it, not about features or price.

## Alternatives

- The new hero copy that shipped with the testimonials did the work, not the
  testimonials.

## Would decide it

The logo-strip variant kept the new hero copy and changed only the proof; it
lifted less than the named testimonials did. That settled it.

## Phase

Entry: the product has its first ten paying users, so social proof is scarce and
each named customer counts.

## Next step
